            📲 Install on your iPhone or iPad

            1. Open this page in Safari.
            2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
            3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

            The Mark Calculator then opens from your home screen like an app, also without internet (uploading frameworks and the AI reader still need internet).

            Important: on iPhone the installed app keeps its own copy of your data, separate from Safari. To bring your modules and marks across, tap Backup in Safari first, then Restore in the app.

            🤖 AI reader (Google Gemini)

            With the AI reader on, every framework you upload is read by Google's Gemini AI. It understands tables, sentences and rules much better than the built-in reader. It's free with your own key.

            1. Go to Google AI Studio and sign in with a Google account.
            2. Click Create API key and copy the key.
            3. Paste it below and click Save.

            Your key is saved only in this browser and is not included in Backup. The text of each framework you upload is sent to Google. On the free tier Google may use it to improve its products, so don't upload anything private. If the AI can't read a file, the built-in reader is used instead.
