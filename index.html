How it works

Add a module from its framework. The app finds every test and assignment with its weight, you check them, and then enter your marks. It shows your final mark so far and what you need to reach your goal.

Privacy

Everything is stored on this device only. Frameworks are read on your phone; with the AI reader on, they are sent to Google to be read. Shared links never include your marks.

Install on your iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

The Mark Calculator then opens from your home screen like an app, also without internet (uploading frameworks and the AI reader still need internet).

Important: on iPhone the installed app keeps its own copy of your data, separate from Safari. To bring your modules and marks across, tap Backup in Safari first, then Restore in the app.

AI reader (Google Gemini)

With the AI reader on, every framework you add is read by Google's Gemini AI. It understands tables, sentences and rules much better than the built-in reader, and can read photos of a framework. It's free with your own key.

  1. Go to Google AI Studio and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Tap Create API key and copy the key.
  3. Paste it below and tap Save.

Your key is saved only on this device and is not included in Backup. Each framework you add is sent to Google. On the free tier Google may use it to improve its products, so don't upload anything private. If the AI can't read a file, the built-in reader is used instead.
